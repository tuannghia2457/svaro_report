10 — Regulatory Context
Personal Data Protection
Luật số 91/2025/QH15
In effect from 1 January 2026. Decree 356/2025/NĐ-CP. AR dossiers contain personal data (contact names, phone, email, bank accounts, signatures). Production systems require data minimisation, access control, encryption, retention policy, and data-subject rights mechanism.
E-Invoice Mandate
Nghị định 254/2026
National e-invoice system fully operational from 1 July 2026. All B2B invoices are structured digital data (XML). Enables deterministic invoice parsing without OCR — a key technical advantage for PayTrace data ingestion.
Hackathon safe approach: Use synthetic / anonymised data only. Read-only workflow. No automated payment demands. No credit decisions. No data transfer to foreign AI/cloud services without legal review.
